Hyeonbeen Lee

I am a first-year PhD student at Virginia Tech, where I'm working with Simon Stepputtis in the Thinking Embodied Agents (TEA) Lab. My research interests include Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, contact-rich manipulation, real-time controller, and sensorless state estimation.

Before joining Virginia Tech, I received my B.Eng. and M.Eng. degrees in Mechanical Engineering from Kyung Hee University, where I was advised by Jin-Gyun Kim in the Modeling & Simulation Lab. During my master's studies, I was also mentored by Hee-Sun Choi at Sejong University and Seongji Han at Chungnam National University. In 2023, I was fortunate to work with Joseph Lim in the CLVR Lab at KAIST.

leehyeonbeen [at] vt.edu  /  CV  /  Scholar  /  ORCiD  /  GitHub

Research

I'm currently developing hierarchical Vision-Language-Action systems and sensorless contact perception methods for real-time robot controllers. Prior to joining Virginia Tech, I worked on data-driven modeling of contact- and vibration-rich physical dynamics at Kyung Hee University.
